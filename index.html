§1 Doctoral analysis · ALICE Run 3

Built end-to-end.

The thesis is the first ALICE Run 3 inclusive charged-particle jet measurement: pp at √s = 13.6 TeV, anti-kT with R = 0.2–0.6, |ηjet| < 0.2. I built the full analysis chain — detector-level QA on continuous-readout data, Run 3-specific cross-section normalization based on the TVX visible cross section, response-matrix construction, Bayesian and SVD unfolding, and a complete systematic-uncertainty program covering tracking-related effects and unfolding stability.

The R-dependent ratios shown above are the principal result. PWG-JE preliminary; presented on behalf of ALICE at EPS-HEP 2025 as the PWG-JE merge talk. Paper proposal targeted August 2026.

§2 Next directions

A coherent program of bias-robust observables
across O+O and Pb+Pb.

Three pillars, year-staggered. Radial observables make selection bias directly testable; photon tagging provides a cleaner recoil probe; together they let us study medium modification and medium response in a controlled way — across the LHC heavy-ion programs and the RHIC small-system data.

Year 1
Jet shape ρ(r) and fragmentation function D(z) in 2025 O+O.
Radial observables that make selection bias directly testable; a natural bridge from the thesis pp baseline into small-system QGP searches.
Years 1 – 2
γ-tagged jet-shape measurements in small systems.
The colorless photon trigger as a cleaner probe of medium-induced jet modification; available where full electromagnetic calorimetry is.
Years 2 – 3
γ+jet Δφ acoplanarity and γ+jet correlations in Pb+Pb.
Medium-induced kT broadening and the diffusion-wake search (arXiv:2408.08599) toward a measurement.
