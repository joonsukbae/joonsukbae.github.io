§2 At a glance

Stage, thesis, evidence.

Stage
PhD candidate at Sungkyunkwan University (advisor: Beomkyu Kim). Defense expected 2026.11–12; degree conferred 2027.02. Available for postdoctoral positions from early 2027.
Thesis
R-dependent charged-particle jet production in pp at √s = 13.6 TeV with ALICE — the first ALICE Run 3 charged-jet publication, in preparation 2026 (paper proposal August 2026).
Collaborations
ALICE (CERN, since 2022.10) · ePIC (BNL, since 2024.02) · LAMPS (RAON, 2022.03–2024.04).
Talks
3 invited workshops · 5 invited collaboration-meeting talks · 6 international contributions (incl. EPS-HEP 2025 on behalf of ALICE, PWG-JE merge talk) · 8 domestic (KPS) · regular PWG-JE speaker since 2023.
Publications
Co-author on ALICE Collaboration papers since 2022.10; first/lead-analyst flagship in preparation. See publications or INSPIRE-HEP.
Software
Authored the PWG-JE cross-section normalization framework used by the SKKU Run 3 charged-jet analyses (pp 13.6 TeV and O+O), and merged a central-framework fix to the track momentum-resolution smearing methodology shared across analyses. 37 PRs (18 merged) to ALICE O²Physics overall.
Detector R&D
ePIC BIC Pb/scintillating-fiber prototype: lead offline analyst at CERN PS T10 (2025.07); on-site DAQ shifter and co-author at KEK AF–AR (2025.03). See Program §3.
Mentoring
Co-mentor of two MSc co-analyzers on the ALICE Run 3 O+O charged-jet measurement; co-organizer and instructor of the KoALICE O²Physics Tutorial (SKKU, January 2024).
