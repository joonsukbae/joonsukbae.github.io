§2 At a glance

Stage, thesis, record.

Stage
PhD candidate at Sungkyunkwan University (advisor: Beomkyu Kim). Defense expected December 2026; degree conferred 2027.02. Available for postdoctoral positions January–March 2027.
Thesis
R-dependent charged-particle jet production in pp at √s = 13.6 TeV with ALICE — the first ALICE Run 3 charged-jet publication, in preparation.
Collaborations
ALICE (CERN, since 2022.10) · ePIC (BNL, since 2024.02) · LAMPS (RAON, 2022.03–2024.04).
Talks
3 invited workshops · 5 invited collaboration-meeting talks · 8 international contributions (incl. EPS-HEP 2025 on behalf of ALICE and Hard Probes 2026) · 9 domestic (KPS) · regular PWG-JE speaker since 2023.
Publications
Co-author on ALICE Collaboration papers since 2022.10; first-author-equivalent thesis paper (main analyzer) in preparation. See publications or INSPIRE-HEP.
Software
Contributor to ALICE O²Physics since 2023: jet-finding and response-matrix tasks, systematic-uncertainty pipelines, particle-level cross-section normalization, and shared track-smearing tools.
Detector R&D
ePIC BIC Pb/scintillating-fiber prototype, three test-beam campaigns: lead analyzer at CERN PS T10 (2025.07); on-site DAQ shifter at KEK AF–AR (2025.03); module performance characterization for the first CERN PS T10 campaign (2024.08), published as a co-author. See Research §3 · Detector R&D.
Mentoring
Co-mentor of the MSc main analyzer on the ALICE Run 3 O+O charged-jet measurement; co-organizer and instructor of the KoALICE O²Physics Tutorial (SKKU, January 2024).
